# ASHEN VECTOR — Asset Option Library

This is a curated source library for an original fast-paced sci-fi arena FPS. It gives Antigravity several asset routes to choose from without silently downloading large packs, mixing uncertain licenses, or treating copyrighted DOOM material as reusable game content.

## How to use

1. Open this folder in Antigravity, or copy it into your game project's root.
2. Read `ASSET_OPTIONS.md` and select one consistent art route (recommended: Route A for a quick prototype).
3. Use the links to download the packs you choose from their creators. Put extracted files in `Downloads_To_Sort/` and ask Antigravity to inspect formats and organize only the assets you selected.
4. Use `ANTIGRAVITY_ASSET_BRIEF.md` as a follow-up prompt once the assets are downloaded.
5. Keep `ASSET_CREDITS.md` updated for anything that enters the shipped build.

## Why this folder contains links and references rather than every pack

Several asset packs are multi-hundred-megabyte archives, and some listings are paid, have free and paid editions, or provide different engine-ready options. This library records the creator links and license status so you can choose and download directly from the source. No paid files, proprietary DOOM files, or assets with unclear reuse terms are included here.

## Reference art

The `Reference_Images/` folder contains reference images already present in the workspace. They depict coastal/lighthouse environments and may be unrelated to this FPS; use them only if they are relevant to your direction. For this game, the art-book and rendering presentation links in `ASSET_OPTIONS.md` are more appropriate visual research sources. Do not copy commercial game art into the project.
